A Fairy Tale - From first call to forever

Akshay & Prashasti

Some love stories take years to unfold, while some just need one phone call. And ours? Well, ours began with a little help from our families and a whole lot of unexpected magic! We met through our relatives, never imagining that one simple phone call would change our lives forever. From the very first conversation, something just clicked. The vibes matched, the connection felt effortless, and it was as if we had known each other for much longer. And in a moment that neither of us could have planned, we found ourselves saying “I Love You” on that very first call! ❤️ A day later, we finally met in person, and somehow, everything felt even more certain. There were no second thoughts, no doubts, just two hearts knowing that they had found something truly special. We knew we didn’t want to wait to become each other’s forever. And here we are today, turning that unexpected beginning into our most beautiful chapter yet. From strangers connected by a phone call to soulmates ready to walk through life hand in hand. One call, one meeting, two hearts, and a lifetime to go. We can’t wait to begin our forever! ♾️❤️
